Apnimed Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update

•
New Drug Application (“NDA”) for AD109, with a proposed proprietary name Oxnimbi™ (aroxybutynin and atomoxetine) tablets, for oral use, remains under U.S. Food and Drug Administration (“FDA”) review with a February 28, 2027, Prescription Drug User Fee Act (“PDUFA”) goal date
•
Commercial readiness activities advance in preparation for a potential U.S. launch
•
Presented five abstracts at CHEST 2026, including two late-breakers, highlighting AD109 Phase 3 clinical data and the burden of OSA
•
$351.8 million in cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026, expected to fund the potential U.S. launch of Oxnimbi and operations into the second quarter of 2028

CAMBRIDGE, Mass., November 16, 2026– Apnimed, Inc. (Nasdaq: APMD) (“Apnimed”), a late-stage clinical pharmaceutical company dedicated to the discovery, development and commercialization of novel oral therapies that address the neurobiology of sleep-related breathing diseases, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

“We are in a transformational period at Apnimed,” said Kevin Lind, Chief Executive Officer of Apnimed. “With our NDA for AD109 (proposed proprietary name Oxnimbi) under FDA review and a PDUFA goal date of February 28, 2027, we are advancing our launch readiness while continuing to strengthen the clinical story. At CHEST, we presented new analyses in postmenopausal women, on baseline hypoxic burden as a potential marker of blood pressure response, and on patient-reported outcomes. We believe this growing body of evidence is important to establishing the potential role of Oxnimbi for the millions of adults with OSA who remain untreated.”

Recent Highlights and Upcoming Milestones

•
The NDA for AD109 (proposed proprietary name Oxnimbi) for the treatment of adults with obstructive sleep apnea (“OSA”) remains under review by the FDA. The FDA has assigned a PDUFA goal date of February 28, 2027.
•
Continued to advance commercial readiness activities, including market access and payer engagement, commercial team and go-to-market build-out, and manufacturing and distribution preparation, to support a potential U.S. launch of Oxnimbi, subject to FDA approval.
•
Presented five abstracts at the American College of Chest Physicians’ CHEST 2026 Annual Meeting: three featuring Phase 3 AD109 data, including two late-breakers and one on patient-reported outcomes, and two real-world studies on the burden of OSA. The meeting also featured a CHEST-led panel discussion on the results of the Phase 3 SynAIRgy and LunAIRo trials and their potential application to OSA management.
•
Completed its upsized initial public offering in August 2026, raising approximately $198.9 million in net proceeds. With $351.8 million in cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026, the Company is well positioned to support commercial readiness and the potential U.S. launch of Oxnimbi, if approved by the FDA.

Third Quarter Financial Results

Cash Position

Apnimed reported cash and cash equivalents of approximately $351.8 million at September 30, 2026.

 

Research and Development Expenses

Research and development expenses were $11.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $2.0 million, or 20%, compared to $9.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The net increase was primarily due to:

•
An increase of $2.1 million in medical affairs expense, driven by increased Key Opinion Leader (“KOL”) engagement in 2026;
•
Higher employee-related expenses of $1.3 million; and
•
Higher stock-based compensation of $0.6 million.

These increases were partially offset by $2.0 million of lower clinical trial expense related to AD109 (proposed proprietary name Oxnimbi), as we focus on commercial launch.

General and Administrative Expenses

General and administrative expenses were $22.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $16.6 million, or greater than 100%, compared to $6.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The net increase was primarily related to:

•
$8.1 million in professional services incurred related to business consulting and contract negotiations, IT infrastructure, and increased salary and related employee expenses due to increased headcount;
•
$4.0 million in infrastructure to prepare the Company, the market, and the Company’s brand for the potential launch of AD109 (proposed proprietary name Oxnimbi), if approved, including key areas such as education on the unmet needs in OSA, pricing strategy, forecast estimates, market access planning, product positioning and stakeholder messaging; and
•
$4.4 million in stock-based compensation related to increased headcount and modifications of certain equity awards.

Other Income

Other income was $2.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $2.4 million, or greater than 100%, compared to $0.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The net increase was primarily related to:

•
A $1.6 million increase in interest income; and
•
A $1.2 million increase in fair value of long-term debt.

These increases were partially offset by a $0.3 million change in fair value of revenue interest liability.

Net Loss

Net loss was $29.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $10.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to the factors impacting the Company’s expenses and other income described above.

About AD109 / Oxnimbi

AD109 is Apnimed’s investigational drug candidate, with a proposed proprietary name Oxnimbi. The proposed name remains subject to final FDA review and acceptance and may change. AD109 is designed to be the first potential pharmacological treatment to improve oxygenation during sleep and target the neuromuscular root cause of upper airway collapse in adults with OSA. It is a potentially first-in-class combination of aroxybutynin, a novel antimuscarinic, and atomoxetine, a selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor. AD109 is intended to be a once-daily oral pill taken at bedtime that is designed to lower the complexity of treating OSA. AD109 may offer an oral solution to help improve oxygenation and health for adults living with OSA. AD109 has completed two Phase 3 clinical trials for the treatment of mild, moderate and severe OSA. Apnimed’s NDA for AD109 is under review by the FDA and was assigned a PDUFA goal

 

date of February 28, 2027. There can be no assurances that AD109 will be approved by the FDA by the PDUFA goal date, or at all.

About Apnimed

Apnimed is a late-stage clinical pharmaceutical company dedicated to the discovery, development and commercialization of novel oral therapies that address the neurobiology of sleep-related breathing diseases. Apnimed believes the introduction of once-nightly oral drugs has the potential to expand diagnosis and the reach of treatment for adults with OSA. Apnimed believes that adults with OSA would benefit from having multiple treatment options with differing mechanisms to more fully address the heterogeneity of OSA’s disease pathophysiology. Apnimed envisions a new era where novel oral therapies simplify intervention and expand the reach of diagnosis and treatment.

Apnimed is advancing its product candidate, AD109 (proposed proprietary name Oxnimbi), which is designed to improve oxygenation in adults living with OSA. Apnimed believes that Oxnimbi could become the catalyst for a new oral treatment paradigm for OSA, which has historically been limited to devices or invasive surgeries.
