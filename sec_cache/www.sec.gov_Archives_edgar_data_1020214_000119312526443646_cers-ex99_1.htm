Cerus Corporation Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Third quarter 2026 total revenue of $64.0 million; third quarter 2026 product revenue of $57.7 million, +10% Y/Y

 

Announced positive topline Phase 3 RedeS results of the INTERCEPT Blood System for red blood cells (RBCs)

CONCORD, CA, November 2, 2026 - Cerus Corporation (Nasdaq: CERS) announced today financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

 

"Our solid performance in Q3 builds upon a strong first half of 2026 and increases our confidence as we approach year-end,” said Vivek Jayaraman, Cerus’s president and chief executive officer. “Continued growth from our IFC business, core platelet franchise and international INT 200 rollout strengthens our foundation as we work to establish INTERCEPT as the global standard of care. The positive topline results we announced today from the Phase 3 RedeS study mark an important milestone as we drive toward extending the benefits of pathogen reduction to red cell transfusions. I’m pleased with our progress and grateful to my colleagues who remain focused on delivering for our customers and the patients they serve.”

Revenue

 

Product revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $57.7 million, compared to $52.7 million for the prior year period, representing year-over-year growth of 10%. Third quarter growth was driven by higher U.S. IFC sales, strength of the U.S. platelet franchise, and INT200 illuminator sales in EMEA.

 

Government contract revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $6.3 million, compared to $7.5 million during the prior year period. The decrease reflects the completion of the Company’s FDA contract in 2025 and the wind-down of the Biomedical Advanced Research and Development Authority (BARDA) 2016 contract which was offset in part by an increase in expenses related to the BARDA 2024 contract.

 

Product Gross Profit & Margin

 

Product gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was $30.3 million, compared to $28.1 million, increasing by 8% over the prior year period. Product gross margin for the third quarter was 52.4% compared to 53.4% in the same period last year. The year-over-year decrease in gross margin was largely driven by a weaker U.S. dollar relative to the euro and higher product costs driven by inflationary pressures offset partially by pricing and favorable product mix shift.

 

Operating Expenses

 

Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $36.2 million, compared to $34.4 million for the same period of the prior year, reflecting a year-over-year increase of 5%.

 

R&D expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $14.3 million, compared to $15.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease primarily reflected lower costs associated with the RedeS study following completion of enrollment, INT200 development following the U.S. PMA submission, and expenses tied to government-funded projects, as reflected in government contract revenue.

 

SG&A expenses totaled $21.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $18.6 million for the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year increase in SG&A expenses was due in part to higher employee-related expenses. In addition, SG&A expenses for Q3 2025 benefited from a one-time tax credit.

 

Net Loss Attributable to Cerus Corporation

 

Net loss attributable to Cerus Corporation for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.6 million, or $0.00 per basic and diluted share, compared to a net loss attributable to Cerus Corporation of $0.02 million, or $0.00 per basic and diluted share, for the same period of the prior year. Net loss attributable to Cerus Corporation for the first nine months of 2026 was $5.1 million, compared to net loss attributable to Cerus Corporation of $13.4 million for the first nine months of 2025.

Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA for the first nine months of 2026 was a positive $12.7 million compared to non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA of positive $6.1 million for the first nine months of 2025.

 

Balance Sheet and Cash Flows

 

At September 30, 2026, the Company had cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments of $57.1 million, compared to $82.9 million at December 31, 2025 and $56.3 million at June 30, 2026.

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $35.0 million outstanding on its term loan and $28.5 million drawn on its revolving credit facility, down from $30.1 million drawn at June 30, 2026. Subject to borrowing base levels, the Company’s revolving line of credit allows for an additional $16.5 million as of September 30, 2026.

 

For the third quarter of 2026, cash generated from operations totaled $3.0 million, compared to $1.9 million generated during the same period of the prior year. Cash generated from operations in the third quarter of 2026 was driven by sequentially improved bottom-line results and inventory management.

 

Reiterating Full-Year 2026 Product Revenue Guidance

 

The Company continues to expect full-year 2026 product revenue to be in the range of $229 million to $231 million, reflecting growth of 11% to 12% from 2025. Included in this range is full-year 2026 IFC revenue guidance of $23 million to $25 million.

ABOUT CERUS

 

Cerus Corporation is dedicated solely to safeguarding the world’s blood supply and aims to become the preeminent global blood products company. Headquartered in Concord,

3

 

 

 

California, the company develops and supplies vital technologies and pathogen-protected blood components to blood centers, hospitals, and ultimately patients who rely on safe blood. The INTERCEPT Blood System for platelets and plasma is available globally and remains the only pathogen reduction system with both CE mark and FDA approval for these two blood components. In the U.S., the INTERCEPT Blood System for Cryoprecipitation is approved for the production of Pathogen Reduced Cryoprecipitated Fibrinogen Complex (commonly referred to as INTERCEPT Fibrinogen Complex), a therapeutic product for the treatment and control of bleeding, including massive hemorrhage, associated with fibrinogen deficiency. The INTERCEPT red blood cell system is under regulatory review in Europe, and in late-stage clinical development in the U.S. For more information about Cerus, visit www.cerus.com and follow us on LinkedIn.
