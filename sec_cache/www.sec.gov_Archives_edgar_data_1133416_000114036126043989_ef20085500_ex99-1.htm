Galectin Therapeutics Reports September 30, 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update
 
NORCROSS, Ga., November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Galectin Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: GALT), the leading developer of therapeutics that target galectin proteins, today reported financial results and provided a business update for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

“New analyses were presented at AASLD, marking the fourth consecutive major liver conference where Galectin data was included in an oral presentation.  We were extremely pleased by the positive feedback received on the oral presentation as well as the two poster presentations and are excited that our data was included in the AASLD conference highlights and the portal hypertension debrief,” said Joel Lewis, Chief Executive Officer and President of Galectin Therapeutics.   “We would like to thank all the authors for their contributions but especially to Dr. Naim Alkhouri for the oral presentation on ANTICIPATE-NASH.”
 
Mr. Lewis continued, “As previously disclosed, we submitted our registrational Phase 3 protocol to FDA in the third quarter after reaching alignment on primary elements of the planned Phase 3 trial design.  Additional information is expected to be presented at the Annual Stockholders’ Meeting on December 3, 2026.”

1
Belapectin Program Highlights
 
Belapectin is a complex carbohydrate drug that targets galectin-3, a critical protein in the pathogenesis of Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) and fibrosis.

MASH Cirrhosis


 •
Previously announced agreement with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) on the path forward for the potential full approval of belapectin. Alignment was reached with the agency on the key elements of the planned Phase 3 trial design, including the primary endpoint of composite liver outcome, blinded central review process for endoscopic assessment of esophageal varices, and the use of a single, 2 mg/kg LBM dose of belapectin.   Registrational Phase 3 trial protocol submission for belapectin in advanced MASH cirrhosis with portal hypertension was filed with FDA in 3Q 2026.


•
Presented oral and poster presentations of new analyses from its global NAVIGATE Phase 2b/3 trial of belapectin at the American Association for the Study of Liver Diseases (AASLD) Annual Meeting in Denver:


o
 
The oral presentation, presented by Naim Alkhouri, MD, FAASLD, Chief Medical Officer of Summit Clinical Research and Director of Steatotic Liver Program at Clinical Research Institute of Ohio, showed the potential utility of ANTICIPATE-NASH risk stratification, a validated noninvasive tool to predict variceal development and assess belapectin treatment effect. In the NAVIGATE trial, belapectin treatment was associated with a favorable shift toward lower ANTICIPATE-NASH predicted clinically significant portal hypertension (CSPH) risk at 18 months, with a net 15.9% placebo-adjusted improvement (p=0.04). Variceal incidence increased with higher CSPH probability yet was consistently lower in patients treated with belapectin vs placebo across all baseline risk categories. The findings suggest that belapectin may reduce CSPH risk and potentially future hepatic decompensation.

 •
Poster of Distinction: Baveno and ELF Risk Stratification in the NAVIGATE Trial


o
 
Baveno VII criteria and ELF score enable risk stratification for CSPH and risk of decompensation, respectively. This Poster of Distinction highlighted the relationship between baseline Baveno VI and ELF risk categories and incidence of varices and belapectin treatment effect.


•
Poster Presentation: Baveno VII and ANTICIPATE-NASH in the Phase 2b GT-026 Trial


o
 
This poster presentation provided an overview of the analysis evaluating the correlation between Baveno VII criteria and ANTICIPATE-NASH, two noninvasive tests, with actual HVPG results the invasive gold standard for portal hypertension measurement. Both noninvasive tests showed moderate agreement with HVPG-defined CSPH in patients with compensated MASH cirrhosis.  Baveno VII demonstrated slightly better concordance and broader risk stratification than ANTICIPATE-NASH. Together, results suggest that noninvasive tools can be used to identify CSPH and enrich the clinical trial population in MASH cirrhosis.

About Galectin Therapeutics

Galectin Therapeutics is dedicated to developing novel therapies to improve the lives of patients with chronic liver disease and cancer. Galectin’s lead drug belapectin is a carbohydrate-based drug that inhibits the galectin-3 protein, which is directly involved in multiple inflammatory, fibrotic, and malignant diseases, for which it has Fast Track designation by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. The lead development program is in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH, formerly known as nonalcoholic steatohepatitis, or NASH) with cirrhosis and portal hypertension, the most advanced form of MASH-related fibrosis. Liver cirrhosis is one of the most pressing medical needs and a significant drug development opportunity. Additional development programs are in treatment of combination immunotherapy for advanced head and neck cancers and other malignancies. Advancement of these additional clinical programs is largely dependent on finding a suitable partner. Galectin seeks to leverage extensive scientific and development expertise as well as established relationships with external sources to achieve cost-effective and efficient development. Additional information is available at www.galectintherapeutics.com.
