Exhibit 99.2 CoStar Group to Acquire Vexcel, Adding the World’s Largest Survey Grade High- Resolution Aerial Imagery Program to Power Richer Search and Discovery Across CoStar with Tens of Billions of New AI-Derived Data Fields Vexcel’s imagery covers more than 45 countries, 320 major global metros and 99.6% of the U.S. Population and is licensed by the world’s largest technology companies ARLINGTON, Va. October 27, 2026 - CoStar Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: CSGP), a leading global provider of online real estate marketplaces, information, analytics, and digital twins in the property markets, announced today that it has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Vexcel Group, Inc. (“Vexcel”), the operator of one of the world’s largest survey-grade aerial imagery programs, for approximately $730 million in cash. Built on decades of investment in aerial capture technology and data processing, Vexcel captures more than 60 million images a year drawn from millions of square kilometers of aerial collection. Its geographic scope spans more than 45 countries and 320 major global metros, covering 99.6% of the U.S. Population. Its comprehensive program includes refreshing major U.S. metros up to three times a year and flying disaster zones within 24 hours of the event – giving up to date intelligence on changes to real estate. Vexcel also designs and manufactures the UltraCam family of aerial cameras and controls the capture technology, the aircraft and the processing pipeline end to end. This enables them to deliver resolutions of 7.5 to 15 centimeters – roughly 16 times finer than typical satellite imagery. It converts that imagery into survey-grade property, parcel, and infrastructure intelligence used for insurance underwriting, catastrophe response, infrastructure planning, digital mapping, location intelligence, and AI applications. Vexcel generated revenue of more than $140 million in the last twelve months, up over 30% the previous twelve months, on recurring multi-year data contracts, with an Adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 20%. “Since 1989, CoStar Group has deployed thousands of researchers in vehicles driving tens of millions of miles across hundreds of cities on multiple continents to find, document and photograph real estate, tenants, and construction,” said Andy Florance, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of CoStar Group. “Since then, we have inspected and photographed millions of properties. This direct observation and capture of on-the-ground facts has differentiated CoStar Group from its competitors, making us the leading CRE information solution in the world.” “Rapid technology advances in aerial photography and lidar are enabling Vexcel to produce extremely high-resolution images and survey-grade measurements of land, houses, office buildings, industrial properties, retail properties, apartment buildings, construction sites, and other infrastructure in the built environment. Combining these high-resolution images with the rapidly evolving capabilities of AI to semantically recognize and extract information and features from properties, we believe we can capture hundreds of data fields per property, across the approximately half a billion properties captured by Vexcel, creating a transformative, new, ultra-low-

 
cost proprietary data source that makes CoStar even more valuable, more accurate, and timelier.” CoStar Group also believes that it can accelerate Vexcel’s revenue growth by growing its sales force and leveraging CoStar Group’s 2,000 salespeople who already have existing relationships with many of Vexcel’s best prospects. “For decades, Vexcel has invested in creating highly accurate geospatial data that customers rely on every day,” said Erik Jorgensen, Chief Executive Officer of Vexcel. “By joining CoStar Group, we gain the scale, technology, and resources to broaden our reach, accelerate innovation, and deliver even greater value to customers worldwide.” Following the closing, Vexcel will continue to operate its data program under the Vexcel brand and to serve its customers and partners worldwide. Erik Jorgensen and the Vexcel leadership team will continue to lead the business. CoStar Group expects to fund the acquisition from cash on hand and its existing revolving credit facility. The acquisition is expected to be accretive to Adjusted EPS in the first full year of ownership and to close in the first quarter of 2027 subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals. Goldman Sachs served as exclusive financial advisor and Latham & Watkins served as legal advisor to CoStar Group.
