EXHIBIT 99.1

The Chemours Company Reports Third Quarter Results

 

Wilmington, Del., November 2, 2026 – The Chemours Company (“Chemours” or “the Company”) (NYSE: CC), a global chemistry company with leading market positions in Thermal & Specialized Solutions (“TSS”), Titanium Technologies (“TT”), and Advanced Performance Materials (“APM”), today announced its financial results for the third quarter 2026.

 

Key Third Quarter 2026 Results & Recent Highlights1

•
Net Sales of $1.5 billion, up slightly from the prior-year quarter, reflecting an approximately 10% year-over-year pricing increase in TT and 26% year-over-year growth in APM Performance Solutions.
•
Net Income attributable to Chemours of $196 million, or $1.29 per diluted share, compared with Net Income attributable to Chemours of $46 million, or $0.31 per diluted share, in the corresponding prior-year quarter.
•
Adjusted Net Income2 of $31 million, or $0.21 per diluted share, compared with Adjusted Net Income of $26 million, or $0.17 per diluted share, in the corresponding prior-year quarter.
•
Adjusted EBITDA2,3 of $200 million, compared with $189 million in the prior-year quarter, driven by improved pricing in TT and growth in APM Performance Solutions.
•
As previously announced, entered into a settlement with the State of North Carolina and 11 local entities to resolve litigation relating to PFAS and other historical discharges from Fayetteville Works, as well as claims of PFAS contamination unrelated to that site, including from the use of AFFF.
•
Formally launching Chemours’ liquid cooling portfolio, including four two-phase direct-to-chip and one immersion cooling fluid under the Faizeon™ liquid cooling brand.
•
Completed the sale of the Kuan Yin property for total net consideration of ~$340 million, including the remaining land parcel for $56 million of gross proceeds.

 

“Our third-quarter results demonstrate strong year-over-year performance in TT and APM, reflecting pricing execution in TiO2 and increased demand across Performance Solutions’ high-value AI infrastructure including data center and semiconductor end markets,” said Denise Dignam, Chemours President and CEO. “We also continued to make meaningful progress under our Pathway to Thrive, including reaching a settlement with the State of North Carolina, driving operational improvements through the Chemours Business System, and closing the sale of the final parcel of land associated with our former Kuan Yin property. While we continue to navigate TSS stationary aftermarket dynamics and a softer macroeconomic environment, we remain focused on disciplined execution of our strategic priorities and the factors within our control to drive long-term returns for shareholders.”

Third quarter 2026 Net Sales were approximately $1.5 billion, an increase of approximately 3% compared with the prior-year quarter. The year-over-year increase in Net Sales was driven by a 5% increase in pricing, partially offset by a 2% decrease in volumes and a slight currency headwind. The pricing increase was primarily driven by previously announced price actions in TT, complemented by a favorable mix shift toward APM Performance Solutions. Lower year-over-year volumes primarily reflected anticipated lower North American stationary AC aftermarket sales of TSS OpteonTM blends compared with elevated demand in the prior-year quarter.

 

Third quarter 2026 Net Income attributable to Chemours was $196 million, or $1.29 per diluted share, compared with $46 million, or $0.31 per diluted share, in the prior-year quarter. Current-year third-quarter Net Income included the receipt of $150 million related to the PFAS insurance proceeds MOU that was executed in connection with the finalization of the judicial consent order ("JCO") with the State of New Jersey. This was presented as a reduction to Selling, general, and administrative expense. Third quarter Adjusted Net Income increased 19% compared with the prior-year quarter, aligned with trends in Adjusted EBITDA as highlighted below.

 

Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was $200 million, compared with $189 million in the prior-year quarter. The increase was driven by pricing momentum in TT and a favorable mix shift toward APM Performance Solutions, partially offset by lower TSS volumes associated with the North American stationary AC aftermarket dynamics noted above and higher costs across each business.

Fourth Quarter 2026 Outlook

 

In the fourth quarter, the Company expects consolidated Net Sales to decrease in the range of 5% to 10%, sequentially, driven by seasonality in TSS and TT, with consolidated Adjusted EBITDA expected to range between $160 million and $185 million. Corporate Expenses are expected to be approximately $40 million to $45 million. The Company also anticipates capital expenditures to be between $75 million and $85 million, with positive Free Cash Flows.

 

TSS expects Net Sales will sequentially decrease in the high-teens to low-twenties percentage range, driven by traditional seasonal patterns related to the North American cooling season. Adjusted EBITDA is expected to be between $105 million and $115 million.

 

TT expects a sequential Net Sales decrease in the high single-digit to low-teens percentage range, driven by seasonality. Adjusted EBITDA is expected to range between $60 million and $65 million.

 

APM expects a sequential Net Sales increase in the low-to-mid teens percentage range, driven by order book strength for Performance Solutions products and certain customer contractual obligations benefiting the fourth quarter. Adjusted EBITDA for APM is expected to be between $40 million and $45 million.

 

Full Year 2026 Outlook

 

The Company expects 2026 Net Sales to grow approximately 2% over 2025, with Adjusted EBITDA between $775 million and $800 million. Capital expenditures are anticipated to be between $220 million and $230 million, with overall Free Cash Flow Conversion above 15%, which includes receipt of $150 million in restricted cash related to the PFAS insurance proceeds MOU in connection with the finalization of the JCO with the State of New Jersey, partially offset by legal settlement payments in the year related to the finalization of the JCO with the State of New Jersey and the announced settlement with the State of North Carolina during the third quarter of 2026 as well as the taxes associated with the Kuan Yin property sale now complete. The Company now anticipates achieving a Net Leverage ratio below 4.2x in 2026.

About The Chemours Company

 

The Chemours Company (NYSE: CC) is a global leader in providing industrial and specialty chemical products for markets, including coatings, plastics, refrigeration and air conditioning, transportation, semiconductor and advanced electronics, general industrial, and oil and gas. Through our three businesses – Thermal & Specialized Solutions, Titanium Technologies, and Advanced Performance Materials – we deliver application expertise and chemistry-based innovations that solve customers’ biggest challenges. Our flagship products are sold under prominent brands such as Opteon™, Freon™, Ti-Pure™, Nafion™, Teflon™, Viton™, and Krytox™. Headquartered in Wilmington, Delaware, and listed on the NYSE under the symbol CC, Chemours has approximately 5,700 employees and 28 manufacturing sites and serves approximately 2,400 customers in approximately 110 countries. For more information, visit chemours.com or follow us on LinkedIn.
