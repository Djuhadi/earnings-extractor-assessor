Braveheart Bio Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

52-week data presented at AHA Scientific Sessions 2026 further strengthen BHB-1893’s potential differentiation, with encouraging clinical activity and support for a highly simplified dosing regimen

LIONHEART-HCM oHCM Phase 3 enrolling with interim analysis expected in second half of 2027; TRUEHEART-HCM fixed-dose oHCM Phase 2b and NOBLEHEART-HCM Phase 3 in nHCM on track to initiate in first half of 2027

Cash, cash equivalents and investments of $503.5 million; cash runway expected into 2029

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 10, 2026 -- Braveheart Bio, Inc. (Nasdaq: BRVE), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing next-generation therapeutics for hypertrophic cardiomyopathy ("HCM") and other serious cardiovascular diseases, today reported unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

“BHB-1893 continues to show a consistent, potentially differentiated profile across multiple clinical studies and our mechanistic work,” said Travis Murdoch, M.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of Braveheart Bio. “The 52-week data presented at AHA showed rapid, deep responses that continued to improve over time, including meaningful improvements in pVO2 and KCCQ-CSS, and evidence supporting a highly simplified dosing regimen. These results inform our updated development strategy, including TRUEHEART-HCM, which will evaluate a fixed dose in oHCM on top of standard of care. With LIONHEART-HCM enrolling, NOBLEHEART-HCM on track to initiate in the first half of 2027 and a strong balance sheet, we are focused on delivering a potential best-in-disease profile with a simple dosing paradigm for patients with HCM.”

Recent Business Highlights

AHA Scientific Sessions 2026 Clinical Data Presentations

•52-Week Open-Label Extension in Obstructive HCM (“oHCM”): Braveheart Bio and partner Hengrui Pharma presented 52-week open-label extension (“OLE”) data from the Phase 2 study of BHB-1893 in 42 patients with oHCM in a late-breaking oral presentation. Mean peak oxygen uptake (“pVO2”) and Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire - Clinical Summary Score (“KCCQ-CSS”) improved by 2.7 mL/kg/min and 17.3 points, respectively, from OLE baseline. By Week 1, before any titration, 67% of patients achieved a Valsalva left ventricular outflow tract gradient (“LVOT-G”) below 30 mmHg; 90% reached this threshold at one or more visits during 52 weeks of treatment. At Week 52, 57% of patients remained on the 40 mg twice-daily starting dose. In an exploratory analysis, gradient and NT-proBNP levels after washout were lower following 52 weeks of treatment than following 12 weeks, consistent with potential reverse remodeling. BHB-1893 was generally well tolerated, with no on-treatment severe or serious adverse events.

•New data supporting differentiated mechanism of action: Braveheart Bio presented preclinical mechanistic data from engineered heart tissue models and an ad hoc pooled analysis of the Phase 2 study of BHB-1893 in non-obstructive HCM (“nHCM”). BHB-1893 improved indices of diastolic function while preserving systolic performance, both in vitro and in patients; at Week 12, a majority of BHB-1893-treated patients had normal septal and lateral e′ velocities (59% and 57%, respectively, compared with 14% and 18% of placebo-treated patients). 52-week data from the nHCM Phase 2 OLE are expected in the first half of 2027.

Development Strategy Update

•On November 6, 2026, Braveheart Bio hosted an investor event, “HCM Spotlight: Advancing a Next-Generation Cardiac Myosin Inhibitor,” featuring leading HCM experts, and outlined its updated clinical development strategy for BHB-1893, which is designed to minimize titration while optimizing efficacy. A replay is available in the Investors section of Braveheart Bio’s website.

•LIONHEART-HCM, Global Phase 3 Trial in oHCM: LIONHEART-HCM is enrolling patients. It is a global, randomized, double-blind, active-comparator-controlled Phase 3 trial evaluating the efficacy and safety of BHB-1893 compared to metoprolol in patients with symptomatic oHCM, using a 40 mg twice-daily starting dose with a single potential titration step to 60 mg at Week 4. The primary endpoint is change in pVO2 from baseline to Week 24; secondary endpoints include LVOT gradient, NT-proBNP and KCCQ-CSS. Results from an interim analysis of LVOT gradient at Week 12 are expected in the second half of 2027.

•Hengrui Pharma’s Study 301, a Phase 3 Trial in oHCM in China: Hengrui Pharma, Braveheart Bio’s collaboration partner in Greater China, has completed enrollment in its Phase 3 trial of HRS-1893 on top of standard of care, with topline results expected in the first half of 2027.

•NOBLEHEART-HCM, Global Phase 3 Trial in nHCM: Braveheart Bio has completed its end-of-Phase 2 meeting with the FDA and plans to initiate NOBLEHEART-HCM in the first half of 2027. NOBLEHEART-HCM is a planned global, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial evaluating BHB-1893 in adults with symptomatic nHCM. The trial design includes a potential single titration step at Week 4. The dual primary endpoints are change in pVO2 and KCCQ-CSS from baseline to Week 36.

•TRUEHEART-HCM, Phase 2b Fixed-Dose Trial in oHCM: TRUEHEART-HCM is a planned Phase 2b trial evaluating a fixed 40 mg twice-daily dose of BHB-1893, with no titration visits, on top of standard of care in patients with symptomatic oHCM. Braveheart Bio has received supportive FDA feedback on the fixed-dose approach and is finalizing the protocol to enable trial initiation in the U.S. in the first half of 2027.

•Braveheart Bio completed its upsized initial public offering in August 2026, selling 24,437,500 shares of common stock, including the full exercise of the underwriters’ option to purchase additional shares, at $18.00 per share for gross proceeds of approximately $439.9 million. Braveheart Bio’s common stock began trading on the Nasdaq Global Market under the ticker symbol “BRVE” on August 6, 2026.


Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Cash, cash equivalents and investments were $503.5 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $89.2 million as of December 31, 2025. Based on the current operating plan, Braveheart Bio estimates that its existing cash, cash equivalents and investments will be sufficient to fund its projected operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements into 2029.

Research and development (R&D) expenses were $17.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to less than $0.1 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily due to clinical and manufacturing costs to support the initiation and enrollment of LIONHEART-HCM, higher personnel-related costs and a $1.0 million milestone under the Hengrui license agreement.

There was no in-process research and development (“IPR&D”) expense for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $58.8 million for the same period in 2025, which related to the in-license of BHB-1893 from Hengrui.

General and administrative (G&A) expenses were $12.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $0.5 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily due to personnel-related costs, including stock-based compensation, and consultant and legal costs to support Braveheart Bio's growth and operations as a public company. G&A expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 included $7.8 million of non-cash stock-based compensation expense, of which $6.1 million related to liability-classified modified executive restricted stock awards that are remeasured to fair value each reporting period.

Net loss was $26.5 million, or $0.51 net loss per share, basic and diluted, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $59.1 million, or $10.05 net loss per share, basic and diluted, for the same period in 2025.

About Braveheart Bio

Braveheart Bio is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing therapies for patients with hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) and other serious cardiovascular diseases. Braveheart Bio’s lead product candidate, BHB-1893, is a next-generation oral small-molecule cardiac myosin inhibitor (CMI) being developed for the treatment of obstructive HCM (oHCM) and non-obstructive HCM (nHCM). Braveheart Bio is aiming to address the limitations of currently available CMIs by designing BHB-1893 for rapid onset, consistent depth of response, preservation of systolic function, prompt reversibility, and a straightforward approach to titration and monitoring. For more information, please visit www.braveheart.bio.
