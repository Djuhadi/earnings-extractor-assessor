NEWS RELEASE
T. ROWE PRICE GROUP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
BALTIMORE (October 29, 2026) - T. Rowe Price Group, Inc. (NASDAQ-GS: TROW) today reported its results for the third quarter of 2026.
▪Assets under management (AUM) of $1.9 trillion
▪Net client outflows of $28.3 billion
▪Diluted earnings per common share (EPS) of $2.79
▪Adjusted diluted EPS of $2.86

Rob Sharps, chair and CEO, commented, “We continue to expand our ETF, SMA, and model portfolio businesses, extending our active investment expertise to more clients through a broader range of portfolio solutions. With innovative product launches, improving investment performance, and continued progress on our strategic partnerships, we are well positioned to continue delivering long-term value for clients and stockholders."

Operating expenses were $1,235.1 million, a decrease of 1.2% from Q3 2025 and 9.6% from Q2 2026. Adjusted operating expenses in Q3 2026 were $1,200.1 million, an increase of 5.8% from Q3 2025 and a decrease of 0.3% from Q2 2026.

▪Compensation, benefits, and related costs in Q3 2026 of $664.7 million increased $32.2 million from Q3 2025 and decreased $11.2 million from Q2 2026. The increase from Q3 2025 was primarily driven by a higher interim bonus accrual and long-term incentive compensation partially offset by lower salaries and temporary personnel costs. The decrease from Q2 2026 was primarily driven by a lower interim bonus accrual and lower employee benefits. The firm employed 7,640 associates at September 30, 2026, a decrease of 2.4% from 7,830 associates at September 30, 2025, and an increase of 1.3% from 7,544 associates at June 30, 2026.

▪Distribution and servicing costs in Q3 2026 of $111.0 million increased $15.2 million from Q3 2025 and $4.9 million from Q2 2026. The increases from prior periods were primarily driven by higher average assets under management distributed through third-party intermediaries.

▪Technology, occupancy, and facility costs in Q3 2026 of $205.1 million increased $7.8 million from Q3 2025 and decreased $0.8 million from Q2 2026. The Q3 2025 and Q2 2026 periods reflect higher technology costs associated with the firm's decision in prior periods to outsource certain technology capabilities, which were partially offset by a reduction in compensation expenses, along with higher costs for cloud-based infrastructure and hosted solutions. The third quarter of 2026 was also impacted by the reversal of about $10 million in accrued technology-related expenses related to work that is expected to be completed in future periods.

▪General, administrative, and other costs in Q3 2026 of $95.5 million increased $7.9 million from Q3 2025 and decreased $9.4 million from Q2 2026. The changes from prior periods were primarily attributable to period-specific items, including a benefit recognized in Q3 2025 and costs recognized in Q2 2026, neither of which recurred in Q3 2026.

▪Restructuring charge in Q3 2026 of $4.0 million relates to actions taken under the previously announced broad and ongoing expense management program, which allows the firm to reduce expense growth and realign resources to support investment in existing and future capabilities.
